Buypal: logo concepts

Three directions for the mark. Each is shown on light and on navy, as an app icon, and at favicon size, with the palette it would lead to. Nothing here copies PayPal's own logo; the family resemblance is only the navy and azure.

Concept A

The Seal

A navy disc with a fine gold ring and a white B whose lower counter is a check mark: a "verified purchase" seal. Reads as trust and finance at a glance. Two-tone wordmark (Buy navy, Pal azure) echoes the way PayPal is set.

Concept B

The Bag & Check

A rounded shopping bag in PayPal-family azure with a navy handle and a white check: buying, done. The most literal and friendly of the three, and the one that looks most like a modern payments app. Lower-case wordmark in a clean geometric sans.

Concept C

The Coin & Companion

An abstract lower-case "b": a tall stem (the "pal", a person) holding a ring (the coin / the payment) with a gold spark. Quiet, editorial and the most premium and least generic of the three, set with a refined serif wordmark and a gold italic "pal". It fits the ivory-and-ink editorial look we already have.
